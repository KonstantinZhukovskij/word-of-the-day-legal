Privacy Policy: Word of the Day

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Word of the Day app (the "App") handles. We keep it short because the App collects very little.

Who we are. The App is published by Konstantin Zhukovskij (an individual developer) ("we", "us"). Contact: konstantinzhukovskij93@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You do not create an account. The App has no sign-in.
  • Your settings, chosen languages and level, and your progress stay on your device.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell or rent your information.
  • We collect limited, anonymous usage and diagnostic information to keep the App working and to improve it.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores on your device: your chosen languages and level, theme, the words you have seen or marked as known, and the state of your Premium access. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes it.

3. Information we collect

We use standard services to understand how the App is used and to fix problems:

  • Anonymous usage statistics. Events such as opening a screen or using a feature. These events do not contain the words you study or your name, email or phone number. An anonymous identifier generated for the installation may be used to count installations and sessions.
  • Diagnostic information. If the App crashes or has an error, a report may be sent. It can include the App version, device model and operating system version, and technical details of the error. It does not include your name or contact details.

We use this information only to operate, maintain and improve the App. It is not used for advertising or for profiling you.

4. Purchases

If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation of the purchase from the store so that we can unlock Premium. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share information only with service providers that process it on our behalf for the purposes above (analytics and crash reporting), and with Apple and Google for purchases. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Retention

Information on your device stays until you delete the App. Anonymous statistics and diagnostic reports are kept by our service providers for a limited period, after which they are deleted or aggregated.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not keep information that identifies you, we usually cannot link any record to a specific person. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To ask a question or make a request, write to konstantinzhukovskij93@gmail.com. You can also limit data collection in your device settings (for example, by resetting or limiting the advertising and analytics identifiers of your device).

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the App or on this page.
